# Production, rehearsal, and promotion plan

## Key decisions

- **Title:** AI for ABM: Setting Up the Foundations
- **Narrative:** Vendor-neutral; Claude is the implementation example.
- **Session length:** Plan for 60 minutes until the organizer confirms otherwise.
- **Primary CTA:** Try one practical ABM skill from the ForgeX repository.
- **Commercial bridge:** Invite teams that need help choosing, governing, or implementing their first workflows to speak with ForgeX. Do not turn the educational close into a sponsorship pitch.

## Rehearsal scorecard

The webinar is ready only when all answers are yes:

- Can Trey state the thesis in under 30 seconds?
- Does each slide make one claim rather than inventorying concepts?
- Can the audience distinguish context, prompt, skill, workflow, agent, MCP, and governance?
- Does the live demo begin producing useful output within 90 seconds?
- Does the demo use only synthetic information?
- Can the demo be abandoned cleanly without derailing the narrative?
- Are all public claims cited or clearly framed as ForgeX recommendations?
- Is the repository accessible to the intended audience?
- Is the educational CTA singular and clear?
- Does the prepared version leave at least eight minutes for questions?
