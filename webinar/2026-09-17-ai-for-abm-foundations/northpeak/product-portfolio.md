# North Peak AI Security product portfolio

## North Peak AI Security Platform

The North Peak AI Security Platform connects AI workloads to their data, identities, infrastructure, owners, policies, exceptions, response paths, and evidence.

The portfolio has three product pillars.

## North Peak Discover

**Job:** build a connected view of the real AI surface.

Approved capability language:

- Map models, agents, data connections, identities, infrastructure, owners, and third-party dependencies
- Connect technical assets to business purpose and operating context
- Surface gaps in ownership and dependency records
- Maintain current inventory and relationship data from approved sources

Do not claim complete discovery across every environment without verified connector and coverage evidence.

## North Peak Protect

**Job:** turn policy into visible controls and accountable decisions.

Approved capability language:

- Connect policy requirements to workloads, identities, data use, and runtime decisions
- Define owners, approvals, exceptions, and response choices
- Apply a shared control model across different environments while preserving local implementation needs
- Make consequential decisions and escalation paths explicit

Do not claim guaranteed prevention, automatic compliance, or universal enforcement.

## North Peak Monitor

**Job:** keep runtime events, changes, exceptions, outcomes, and evidence review-ready.

Approved capability language:

- Track material changes and control events
- Preserve decision context, ownership, exceptions, and outcomes
- Coordinate response across security, infrastructure, application, and risk teams
- Support continuous review and evidence preparation

Do not claim that monitoring removes the need for incident response, audit judgment, or human review.

## Portfolio story

```text
DISCOVER the connected AI surface
      ↓
PROTECT decisions in the workflow
      ↓
MONITOR change, response, and evidence
      ↺
Feed the learning back into discovery and policy
```

## Standard first engagement

Choose one consequential AI workload and map:

1. business purpose;
2. models and agents;
3. data, identity, infrastructure, and third parties;
4. owners and policies;
5. exceptions and response choices;
6. evidence requirements.

This is an approved fictional next step. It is not a promise of a specific implementation outcome.
