## Prohibited claims

- Real customer names, logos, deployments, endorsements, or results
- Guaranteed risk reduction, prevention, compliance, or uptime
- Unqualified claims of complete visibility or universal control coverage
- Financial or performance metrics that are not explicitly labeled as synthetic demonstration data
- Claims that an account is in market, has intent, or has adopted a named technology without approved evidence

## Evidence labels

Use these labels in every account brief and generated asset:

- **Observed:** supplied or verified evidence with a source location
- **Hypothesis:** a reasonable interpretation that requires validation
- **Recommendation:** a proposed action based on evidence and judgment
- **Unknown:** missing information that could change the recommendation
- **Approval:** a decision that belongs to a named human owner

## Approval gates

Human approval is required before:

- adding a customer or performance claim;
- using a target's logo or brand assets;
- publishing a campaign experience;
- sending outreach;
- writing to CRM or another system of record;
- spending budget;
- granting a tool write permission.
