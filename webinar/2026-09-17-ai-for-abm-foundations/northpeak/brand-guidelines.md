# North Peak AI Security brand guidelines

**Status:** Approved fictional demonstration canon

**Applies to:** ForgeX webinar examples, slides, synthetic campaign assets, and skill demonstrations

## Brand idea

North Peak makes complex AI security systems feel clear, connected, and operational. The brand should communicate control without fearmongering and technical depth without visual clutter.

## Visual identity

### Core palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Night | `#04191C` | Primary dark background, high-trust foundation |
| Ink | `#0C1A2B` | Body copy and structured information |
| Spruce | `#0C7C5A` | Primary brand accent and navigation |
| Emerald | `#0FA875` | Action, progress, and active control |
| Mint | `#E2F3EB` | Soft information surfaces |
| Cloud | `#F1FAF6` | Light section background |
| White | `#FBFDFB` | High-contrast text and clean space |
| Sky | `#9CE1EB` | Secondary data and connectivity accent |
| Signal Lime | `#8CE9C2` | Sparing signal, success, or active-state accent |

The Alder Manufacturing campaign may introduce copper `#D98555` as a target-account accent. It must not replace North Peak's emerald brand system.

### Typography

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque, with Sora as an acceptable fallback
- Body and interface: Manrope, with Avenir Next or a system sans as fallback
- Use sentence case for headlines
- Keep long technical passages in a highly readable body style

### Logo and mark

The core mark is an emerald mountain form. Preserve clear space, high contrast, and the original aspect ratio. Do not combine it with a customer logo in a way that implies a partnership or endorsement.

### Shape and layout

- Use strong horizontal structure, wide margins, and restrained rounded corners
- Favor connected maps, paths, and evidence records over generic shield imagery
- Use dark operational surfaces for system views and light editorial surfaces for explanation
- Let one bright signal carry meaning. Do not decorate every element with accent color

### Imagery

Prefer:

- real operational environments;
- infrastructure, industrial, clinical, and cloud settings;
- people collaborating around systems and decisions;
- abstract maps that show identity, data, models, agents, and infrastructure as one system.

Avoid:

- hooded-hacker clichés;
- glowing padlocks and generic cybersecurity stock art;
- humanoid robots as the default metaphor for AI;
- imagery that implies a real customer relationship.

## Voice

North Peak is calm, specific, and operational.

Use:

- active language;
- short declarative claims;
- concrete nouns such as models, agents, data paths, identities, controls, owners, and evidence;
- a clear connection between protection and business continuity.

Avoid:

- fear, doom, or breach theater;
- unexplained acronyms;
- claims of complete visibility or guaranteed prevention;
- vague transformation language.

## Canonical lines

- Secure every AI decision, wherever it runs.
- The fastest AI programs make security context part of every decision, not a review after the fact.
- Evidence should be a by-product of the control, not a scramble after the incident.

These are approved fictional brand lines. They are not performance claims.

## Accessibility

- Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive elements
- Do not use color as the only indicator of status
- Provide useful alt text for diagrams and images
- Keep text at readable sizes in slides and campaign assets
