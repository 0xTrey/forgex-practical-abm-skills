# North Peak AI Security ideal customer profile

## Best-fit account

North Peak is designed for enterprises whose AI programs cross multiple technical and organizational boundaries.

### Firmographic indicators

- Large or complex mid-market and enterprise organizations
- Distributed cloud, edge, plant, clinical, or application environments
- Multiple teams involved in AI delivery, infrastructure, security, privacy, or risk
- Regulated or operationally sensitive workflows

### Operational indicators

- Models or agents are moving from pilots into production
- AI workloads depend on sensitive data, third parties, edge systems, or critical infrastructure
- Ownership is fragmented across security, platform, application, data, and risk teams
- Evidence is assembled manually after a review, event, or audit request
- Different environments use different controls without a common decision record

### Timing signals

- Enterprise AI platform or agent rollout
- New Head of AI Security, CISO, AI governance leader, or infrastructure transformation owner
- Public expansion of industrial, clinical, customer-facing, or operational AI
- Audit, regulatory, or board-level attention on AI risk
- Consolidation of cloud, data, identity, or security architecture

## Strong vertical contexts

- Manufacturing and industrial operations
- Healthcare and life sciences
- Financial services and insurance
- Technology and cloud infrastructure
- Energy, logistics, and other distributed operations

Vertical membership alone is not evidence of fit. The account should show operational complexity and a real AI delivery motion.

## Disqualifiers

- No meaningful AI use beyond individual productivity tools
- No cross-functional owner or sponsor for AI risk and delivery
- A request for guaranteed compliance, guaranteed prevention, or unsupported performance claims
- A project that requires invasive personal data or unapproved access
- A target selected only because it is large or well known

## Qualification questions

1. Which AI workloads matter most to business or operational continuity?
2. Where do those workloads run, and what data and identities do they depend on?
3. Who approves policy, exceptions, response, and evidence?
4. What changes between design review and runtime?
5. How is evidence assembled today?
6. What would move faster if security context were part of the workflow?

## ABM segmentation

| Segment | Evidence threshold | Recommended motion |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic account | Multiple verified timing and complexity signals | Named-account research, buying-group map, executive point of view, tailored experience |
| Priority cluster | Shared vertical or operating pattern plus at least one current signal | Cohort campaign with role and industry paths |
| Education | Category fit without verified timing | Point-of-view content and self-guided diagnostic |
| Hold | Weak fit, restricted evidence, or no credible problem | No personalized campaign |
