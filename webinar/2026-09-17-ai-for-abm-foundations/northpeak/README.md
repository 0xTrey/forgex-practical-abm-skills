# North Peak AI Security context corpus

North Peak AI Security is the fictional seller used throughout the ForgeX AI for ABM webinar. This folder is the canonical, portable company brain for the demonstration.

## What belongs here

- [`brand-guidelines.md`](brand-guidelines.md): visual and verbal brand rules
- [`messaging-framework.md`](messaging-framework.md): positioning, message pillars, claims, and objections
- [`icp.md`](icp.md): account fit and disqualification logic
- [`personas.md`](personas.md): role-level jobs, tensions, proof needs, and messages
- [`product-portfolio.md`](product-portfolio.md): approved product architecture and capability language
- [`proof-and-claims.md`](proof-and-claims.md): claim permissions and evidence rules
- [`campaign-demo-brief.md`](campaign-demo-brief.md): synthetic Alder Manufacturing evidence for the webinar
- [`source-manifest.md`](source-manifest.md): origins, owners, and freshness status

## Rules

1. Everything in this folder is fictional and for demonstration use.
2. No customer, performance, financial, or security outcome may be presented as real.
3. Account observations and role hypotheses must remain clearly labeled.
4. Changes to product language must be reflected in the product portfolio, messaging framework, and proof register.
5. If two files conflict, stop and resolve the conflict in the source manifest before producing external material.

## Why this structure is portable

The files are plain Markdown. A team can use them in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, Codex, another approved harness, or a retrieval system without rewriting the company knowledge into a vendor-specific format.

The company brain should describe the business. Tool configuration should describe how a particular environment reads it. Keep those concerns separate.
