# Synthetic ABM demo input

All companies, people, facts, and proof points below are fictional and exist only for the September 17 ForgeX demonstration.

## Seller

**Brightwave Analytics** is a fictional B2B marketing analytics company. Its fictional product helps enterprise marketing teams connect campaign activity, account engagement, opportunity context, and executive reporting in one governed measurement workspace.

Approved fictional capabilities:

- Unifies approved campaign and CRM data into account-level reporting.
- Supports role-based dashboards and audit history.
- Helps marketing and revenue teams define shared measurement logic.
- Produces drafts and recommendations but does not autonomously change CRM records.

Approved fictional proof:

- In a synthetic pilot, a fictional enterprise team reduced monthly ABM reporting preparation from five business days to one.
- The proof may be described only as a fictional demonstration result.

## Target account

**Northwind Industrial Systems** is a fictional global manufacturer with approximately 4,000 employees.

Approved fictional account observations dated September 1, 2026:

- Northwind announced a new enterprise growth program focused on three strategic industries.
- Its marketing organization is hiring a Director of Account-Based Marketing and a Senior Marketing Operations Manager.
- The fictional ABM job description emphasizes account prioritization, sales alignment, industry plays, and executive measurement.
- The fictional marketing-operations job description emphasizes CRM data quality, campaign governance, and shared reporting.
- Northwind's fictional annual report says inconsistent regional reporting slows executive decisions.

## Campaign objective

Create an approval-ready account campaign brief showing how Brightwave could help Northwind establish shared ABM measurement for its industry growth program.

Desired next action: a 30-minute working session to compare Northwind's current ABM measurement process with a simple shared-account reporting model.
