## Interaction script

### Opening chat prompt

> What is one repeated ABM job your team keeps rebuilding from scratch?

Yael or the moderator should group answers into research, planning, content, activation, and reporting. Trey can reference the largest cluster during Slide 20.

### Maturity self-rating

Ask attendees to hold up zero to five fingers privately or use the platform poll if available. The purpose is orientation, not competition.

### Automation or agent challenge

Read this scenario:

> Every morning, a system pulls the same report, runs the same prompt, writes the same summary, and posts it to the same channel.

Answer: useful automation, not an agent. It follows a predetermined path.

Then read:

> The system receives a campaign goal, inspects account evidence and engagement, chooses which skills and tools to use, decides what artifact is needed, evaluates it, and asks for approval before activation.

Answer: agentic system, assuming the dynamic behavior is real and governed.

He will connect prompts, context, MCP, and agents through one practical ABM job, then build a fresh Claude workspace live using the ForgeX resources you will receive after the session.

## Moderator close

> We will share the ForgeX maturity rubric, field guide, North Peak example, and practical ABM skills with the recording. If your next move is from Level 4 to Level 5, use the follow-up link to book a one-to-one architecture working session around your systems, goals, data, and approval boundaries.

## Timing fallback

If the teaching section runs long, preserve Slides 3, 5, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 20, and 21. Compress the other levels into Slide 18.

Do not reduce the fresh-workspace demo below 12 minutes. The public promotion promises a live setup, and the build is where the portability claim becomes believable.
