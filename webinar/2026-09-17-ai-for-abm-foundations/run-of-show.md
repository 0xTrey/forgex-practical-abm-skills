| Time | Segment | Slides | Presenter job |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Welcome and promise | 1 | Establish that this is an operating-system session, not a tool comparison. |
| 0:03–0:07 | The real problem | 2 | Name the gap between clever AI output and repeatable ABM work. |
| 0:07–0:13 | The five-layer foundation | 3–4 | Give the audience the core mental model and architecture. |
| 0:13–0:19 | The central brain | 5 | Explain why shared, versioned context matters. |
| 0:19–0:25 | Prompt versus skill | 6–7 | Show when a prompt becomes reusable operating infrastructure. |
| 0:25–0:31 | Workflow versus agent | 8 | Make the autonomy tradeoff concrete. |
| 0:31–0:36 | What MCP is for | 9 | Explain connections, permissions, and why MCP is not the strategy. |
| 0:36–0:40 | Human review gates | 10 | Put approval before send, publish, spend, and system writes. |
| 0:40–0:44 | ABM application map | 11–12 | Connect the foundation to discover, plan, build, activate, and learn. |
