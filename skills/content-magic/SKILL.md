# Content Magic

Read [`../../shared/SAFETY.md`](../../shared/SAFETY.md) and [`../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md`](../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md) before starting.

## Outcome

Turn an approved report, webinar, video, deck, article, guide, or transcript into a structured campaign experience with a clear narrative, useful content paths, derivative assets, and traceable claims.

## Use when

- A substantial approved source asset deserves more reach or a better buyer experience.
- The team needs an interactive page, campaign hub, or channel-ready derivative pack.
- The source contains enough substance to support multiple useful moments.

## Do not use when

- The source is unapproved, outdated, private beyond the intended audience, or lacks reuse permission.
- The user wants invented findings, quotations, customer proof, or product capabilities.
- The user expects automatic publication or distribution.

## Inputs

Required:

1. Approved source asset.
2. Audience and campaign objective.
3. Desired experience or derivative formats.
4. Brand voice and approved CTA.

Optional: campaign brief, proof library, destination platform, content taxonomy, analytics plan, and examples of preferred interaction patterns.

## Workflow

1. Confirm source approval, audience, objective, formats, CTA, confidentiality, and attribution requirements.
2. Build a source truth sheet: thesis, key messages, facts, quotes, examples, caveats, and source locations.
3. Identify the audience's most useful content journey rather than reproducing the source's original order.
4. Create an experience architecture with one primary narrative and optional topic, role, or stage paths.
5. Draft page copy and derivative content with source IDs attached to every factual claim.
6. Design interaction and visual recommendations that support comprehension rather than novelty.
7. Produce the requested wireframe, copy deck, platform specification, code draft, or derivative pack.
8. QA source fidelity, attribution, accessibility, brand fit, channel differentiation, links, and CTA.
9. Stop before publishing, scheduling, emailing, or overwriting the source.

## Output contract

Return:

- `source-truth-sheet.md` with evidence IDs;
- `content-experience-brief.md` and journey map;
- page outline, copy deck, and requested draft format;
- derivative content plan or drafts when requested;
- source and attribution register;
- QA report and human approvals required.

## Quality checks

- Every factual claim and quotation is traceable.
- The experience has a distinct audience job and narrative.
- Derivatives are channel adaptations, not shortened duplicates.
- Caveats and source meaning are preserved.
- No confidential information or invented proof appears.
- Nothing was published, scheduled, or sent.

## Quick-start prompt

> Use Content Magic on the attached approved `[ASSET TYPE]` for `[AUDIENCE]`. Turn it into `[EXPERIENCE OR FORMATS]`, preserve source truth, cite claims, and stop at review-ready drafts.
