# Context corpus template

Use this as the minimum structure. Keep files concise enough for a person to review.

## README.md

- Purpose
- Intended users and tools
- In-scope business units or products
- Prohibited content
- File map
- Conflict protocol
- Update workflow

## brand-guidelines.md

- Brand idea
- Visual tokens and asset rules
- Typography and layout
- Voice and language
- Approved lines
- Prohibited treatments
- Accessibility

## messaging-framework.md

- Positioning
- Category point of view
- Problem, value, differentiation, proof, next step
- Audience messages
- Objections and responses
- Preferred and prohibited language

## icp.md

- Best-fit characteristics
- Operational indicators
- Timing signals
- Disqualifiers
- Qualification questions
- Segmentation logic

## personas.md

For each role:

- job to be done;
- tensions;
- questions;
- proof needs;
- useful message;
- language to avoid.

## product-portfolio.md

For each product or service:

- customer job;
- approved capability language;
- boundaries and dependencies;
- prohibited or conditional claims;
- recommended next step.

## proof-and-claims.md

- Approved claims with source and scope
- Conditional claims and required qualification
- Prohibited claims
- Customer and asset permissions
- Evidence labels
- Human approval gates

## source-manifest.md

| Source | Owner | Permission | Access date | Freshness | Status | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## validation-report.md

- Test task
- Expected behavior
- Actual behavior
- Contradictions
- Missing context
- Noise or retrieval problems
- Correction made
- Final readiness: `Ready`, `Incomplete`, or `Blocked`
