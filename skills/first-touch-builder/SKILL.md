# First-Touch Builder

Read [`../../shared/SAFETY.md`](../../shared/SAFETY.md) and [`../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md`](../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md) before starting.

## Outcome

Create a short, human-sounding first-touch draft that connects one genuine observation to one relevant problem, one credible reason to respond, and one low-friction CTA.

## Use when

- A marketer or seller has an approved contact, offer, and legitimate reason to reach out.
- The opening can be grounded in a real relationship or relevant professional signal.
- The user wants an unsent email or LinkedIn draft for review.

## Do not use when

- The contact is suppressed, stale, unverified, or lacks a legitimate outreach basis.
- Personalization relies on family, health, politics, finances, protected traits, or unrelated personal details.
- The user wants automated sending, deceptive threading, manufactured urgency, or fabricated familiarity.

## Inputs

Required:

1. Contact name, professional role, company, and relationship context.
2. One verified observation or signal relevant to the offer.
3. One offer and one desired CTA.
4. Approved proof point or credibility statement.
5. Suppression and communication constraints.

Optional: prior correspondence, preferred channel, voice samples, calendar link, subject-line style, and regional compliance guidance.

## Workflow

1. Confirm contact eligibility, relationship context, offer, CTA, evidence, and prohibited topics.
2. Select the strongest relevant opener. If no credible opener exists, use a direct professional reason rather than fake personalization.
3. Build the message as `Observation → relevance → proof → one ask`.
4. Write in the sender's natural voice. Prefer concrete nouns and short sentences over marketing language.
5. Keep one offer and one CTA. Do not bundle sponsorship, advisory, events, training, and certification.
6. Create up to three subject lines for email or one connection line for LinkedIn.
7. Fact-check the opener, company, role, proof, links, and calendar destination.
8. Mark assumptions and alternatives for human review.
9. Stop with unsent drafts. Do not create or launch a sequence unless separately requested and approved.

## Output contract

Return:

- primary first-touch draft;
- two alternate openings when evidence supports them;
- subject lines or LinkedIn connection line;
- personalization evidence and source;
- proof and link check;
- compliance, suppression, and human-review notes.

## Quality checks

- The opener is true, relevant, and professionally appropriate.
- The message contains one offer and one CTA.
- The proof is approved and accurately stated.
- The draft sounds like a person who knows why they are writing.
- Links and recipient details are verified.
- Nothing was sent or placed into an active sequence.

## Quick-start prompt

> Use First-Touch Builder for `[CONTACT]` at `[COMPANY]`. Our relationship is `[CONTEXT]`; the verified signal is `[SIGNAL]`; the offer is `[ONE OFFER]`; the proof is `[APPROVED PROOF]`; and the CTA is `[ONE ASK]`. Draft the message and stop before sending.
