# One-to-One Microsite

Read [`../../shared/SAFETY.md`](../../shared/SAFETY.md) and [`../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md`](../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md) before starting.

## Outcome

Create a draft named-account buyer experience that connects verified account context to the seller's point of view, proof, resources, and next step. The experience must feel researched without pretending to be owned or endorsed by the target account.

## Use when

- A named account, offering, objective, and approved account brief exist.
- The seller has enough relevant proof and content to justify a dedicated experience.
- A human owner will review claims, personalization, and sharing.

## Do not use when

- The goal is to mimic the target's website or imply target endorsement.
- Personalization depends on private-person data, speculation, or sensitive traits.
- The user expects automatic publication, account contact, or CRM updates.

## Inputs

Required:

1. Seller brand and offering.
2. Approved named-account campaign brief.
3. Primary buying-group job and CTA.
4. Approved seller proof and content.
5. Account evidence with source links or file locations.

Optional: known professional stakeholders, active conversation context, destination platform, analytics plan, and access requirements.

## Workflow

1. Confirm seller and target identities, offering, audience, CTA, sources, and sharing boundary.
2. Build a personalization ledger: verified account fact, relevance to the offering, permitted page use, and source.
3. Remove decorative personalization that does not improve the buyer's decision.
4. Create a story arc from account context to useful insight, seller relevance, proof, resources, and next step.
5. Design the smallest useful page architecture and optional role-based paths.
6. Draft copy that clearly speaks from the seller; do not use the target's first-person voice.
7. Produce the requested wireframe, copy deck, platform specification, or accessible code draft.
8. QA every personalized claim, logo use, link, CTA, responsive behavior, access rule, and analytics specification.
9. Stop at a private preview or local draft. Do not publish or share with the account.

## Output contract

Return:

- `one-to-one-brief.md` and personalization ledger;
- page outline and copy deck;
- requested draft or platform specification;
- source, proof, content, and asset register;
- QA report including impersonation and privacy checks;
- sharing and publication decisions requiring approval.

## Quality checks

- Every account-specific claim is verified and useful.
- Seller and target branding are clearly distinguished.
- Target marks are not used to imply endorsement.
- The experience helps a buying group make a decision.
- Access, analytics, and privacy requirements are visible.
- No page was published or shared externally.

## Quick-start prompt

> Use One-to-One Microsite for `[SELLER]` and `[TARGET ACCOUNT]` using the approved brief and proof. Create a relevant, non-impersonating buyer experience draft and stop before publication or account sharing.
