# Industry Campaign Page

Read [`../../shared/SAFETY.md`](../../shared/SAFETY.md) and [`../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md`](../../shared/COMPATIBILITY.md) before starting.

## Outcome

Create a one-to-few campaign-page draft for a specific industry, sub-industry, persona cohort, or account cluster. The adaptation should change the problem framing, evidence, language, and content path—not merely swap the industry name.

## Use when

- A base campaign and a coherent vertical or cohort are defined.
- Approved industry evidence and relevant proof are available.
- Several similar accounts need a shared experience before one-to-one investment is warranted.

## Do not use when

- The cohort is based on sensitive personal traits or weak stereotypes.
- The only customization is an industry label with no evidence or message change.
- The user expects invented regulations, benchmarks, customer proof, or automatic publication.

## Inputs

Required:

1. Approved base campaign brief and offer.
2. Cohort definition and inclusion logic.
3. Industry problems, language, and evidence sources.
4. Brand system, approved proof, and CTA.

Optional: sample accounts, regulations, role priorities, industry content, competitor context, and destination platform.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the cohort definition, shared business condition, exclusions, and desired action.
2. Build an evidence table for industry priorities, language, constraints, and proof.
3. Identify what stays constant from the base campaign and what must change for this cohort.
4. Write a cohort-specific message hierarchy and content path.
5. Create a page architecture that supports shared relevance plus optional persona or topic branching.
6. Draft copy and visuals without implying that every company in the industry is identical.
7. Produce a wireframe, copy deck, platform specification, or accessible code draft.
8. QA evidence, generalization risk, proof relevance, mobile flow, links, and CTA consistency.
9. Stop before publication or audience activation.

## Output contract

Return:

- `industry-campaign-brief.md` with cohort logic and evidence;
- `base-to-cohort-message-map.md` showing what changed and why;
- page outline, copy deck, and requested draft format;
- source, proof, and asset register;
- QA report and unresolved human decisions.

## Quality checks

- The cohort shares a defensible business condition.
- Industry claims are sourced and current.
- The copy contains meaningful adaptation beyond name substitution.
- Differences inside the cohort are acknowledged where material.
- Proof is relevant to the selected audience.
- No page was published and no audience was activated.

## Quick-start prompt

> Use Industry Campaign Page to adapt the attached campaign for `[INDUSTRY OR COHORT]`. Ground the page in approved evidence, show what changed from the base story, and stop at a responsive draft.
